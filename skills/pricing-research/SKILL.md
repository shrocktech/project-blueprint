# Research Pricing and Refund Terms

Confirm the target project. Plain filenames refer to its instructions, not files beside this installed skill; during adoption use the blueprint's template equivalents. Read `offering.md` and applicable `legal.md` rules. Reviews do not start implementation queue work; authorized edits follow `git-workflow.md`.

1. Inspect the product, existing commercial decisions, billing configuration, published policies, provider costs, and intended customers. Reuse known facts; ask only material missing questions. Identify the purchase model and comparable scope before searching.
2. Research roughly 3–5 relevant alternatives, or explain a smaller useful set. Prefer official product, pricing, cancellation, and refund pages. Keep a compact comparison in private `docs/internal/` with direct URLs and access dates; mark unavailable or ambiguous information as unknown. Do not infer “no refunds” from a missing policy.
3. Normalize the comparison: currency, billing interval, introductory versus renewal prices, seats/sites/usage, included features, limits, support, taxes/mandatory fees, trials, and cancellation/refund conditions where applicable. Show original prices alongside conversions and their dated basis. Explain material differences rather than presenting unlike plans as equivalents.
4. Estimate sustainable pricing using realistic usage, provider/payment costs, support, and margin assumptions alongside customer value and positioning. Check heavy-use exposure; distinguish evidence from estimates. Use available customer feedback or willingness-to-pay evidence, and flag its absence. Competitor habits do not establish legal requirements; route applicability questions through `legal.md`.
5. Present a brief recommendation as A, with useful B/C alternatives, tradeoffs, assumptions, and a defensible starting range. Address applicable refund windows, eligibility, initial purchases versus renewals, consumed services, exclusions, and the request process. Explain cancellation separately. Avoid inventing mandatory tiers, discounts, or guarantees. Ask the material business decision using `git-workflow.md`; allow the owner's own answer.
6. Record the decision in the commercial record required by `offering.md`. For authorized implementation, compare each customer-facing surface with that record and verify billing/refund behavior using synthetic data and provider test modes. Report discrepancies and resolve them before release. Recommend a proportionate review after real sales, costs, or market conditions provide new evidence; research does not guarantee optimal pricing or activate monitoring.
