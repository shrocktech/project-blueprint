# Offering Rules

Apply before customer-facing pages or initial pricing, during launch preparation, and whenever the offer changes, including after launch. Keep copy brief and understandable to the intended audience.

- Explain the audience, problem, supported benefits, and next action. Use verified capabilities and current screenshots; distinguish available features from plans. Pre-launch pages must accurately identify waitlists, previews, and availability.
- Substantiate measurable claims, comparisons, testimonials, and trust marks. Never invent customers, guarantees, savings, scarcity, or certifications. Commitments and disclosures follow [legal.md](legal.md); verify data/security claims under [security.md](security.md#security-review). Presentation follows [design.md](design.md). Verify links and advertised signup/purchase journeys, including mobile.

Use the `pricing-research` skill recorded in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before initial pricing or material commercial changes. Compare relevant products, customer value, delivery/support costs, and applicable constraints. Competitor prices are evidence, not a target or proof of an optimal price. Assume no subscription tiers, discounts, or universal refund window.

- Maintain one approved commercial record indexed from private `docs/internal/` under [documentation.md](documentation.md): audience, offering, rationale, effective version, and approval. Link canonical billing configuration and approved policies instead of duplicating them. Separate proposals from active terms.
- The owner or explicitly authorized business delegate approves initial prices and material changes; reuse recorded decisions. Research does not authorize live billing changes. Record effects on existing customers, preserving commitments and required notices under `legal.md`.
- Research refund eligibility, deadlines, renewals, consumed services, and cancellation separately. Policy wording, applicability review, and commitments follow `legal.md`; approved public policies belong in `docs/public/legal/`.
- Keep landing pages, feature/plan comparisons, pricing pages, checkout, billing, and customer docs consistent with approved amounts, intervals, currencies, limits, charges, trials, renewals, cancellation, and refunds. Update affected drafts before issue completion; activate with the corresponding authorized offering under [launch.md](launch.md). Free products need only applicable rules.

When substantive marketing work begins, ask the developer whether to install selected [Corey Haines marketing skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills), explain relevant choices, and record the decision. Never download during adoption or without approval. Honor recorded choices without repeated prompts; deferral does not block work. Before approved installation, review selected skills, dependencies, license, and project fit; record the installed revision and location.
