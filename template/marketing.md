# Marketing Rules

Apply before the first customer-facing page, during launch preparation, and whenever an issue changes the offer. Keep copy brief, specific, and understandable to the intended audience.

- Explain who the product serves, the problem it solves, supported benefits, and a clear next action. Use verified capabilities and current screenshots; distinguish available features from planned ones.
- Keep landing pages, feature/plan comparisons, pricing, checkout, and customer documentation consistent with approved commercial facts under [pricing.md](pricing.md). Update affected drafts before issue completion; publish with the corresponding authorized offering.
- Substantiate measurable claims, comparisons, testimonials, and trust marks. Never invent customers, guarantees, savings, scarcity, or certifications. Follow [legal.md](legal.md) for commitments and disclosures, and [design.md](design.md) for presentation.
- Verify page links and the advertised signup/purchase journey, including mobile presentation. Pre-launch pages must accurately identify waitlists, previews, and availability.

When substantive marketing work begins, ask the developer whether to download/install selected skills from [Corey Haines's Marketing Skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills). Explain the relevant choices and record the decision. Do not download during blueprint adoption or without approval; deferral does not block this guide. Honor recorded choices without repeated prompts. Before approved installation, review the selected skills, dependencies, license, and project fit; record the installed revision and location.
