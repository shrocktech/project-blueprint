# Legal and Privacy Rules

Record business locations, customer/service regions, and product scope in private project docs. Determine applicable jurisdictions from those facts; assume no country or regional exemption. Reassess when operations, targeting, customers, or relevant law change. Use current official sources rather than importing unrelated regional boilerplate.

Use the `legal-review` skill at the location recorded in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for initial policies, material policy changes, or uncertain applicability. Read [security.md](security.md) for technical safeguards.

- Write brief, plain-language policies describing verified practices. Keep applicable disclosures; brevity is not permission to omit them. Avoid unrelated boilerplate and blanket compliance claims.
- Never promise perfect security. Claims about confidentiality, sharing, deletion, retention, uptime, refunds, or response deadlines need verified operational support and authorization. Distinguish legal duties from optional promises; never conceal gaps or silently weaken existing commitments.
- Assess privacy notices before collecting real personal information, including waitlists and betas. Terms, cookie/tracking disclosures and controls, subscription/refund terms, and acceptable-use rules depend on the product; do not create every policy or a consent banner automatically.
- Keep applicable public policy sources in `docs/public/legal/` under [documentation.md](documentation.md). Keep the supporting data inventory, applicability research, and unresolved questions private in `docs/internal/`. Preserve existing approved policies during adoption; no automatic replacement or publication.
- Review affected policies whenever an issue changes data handling or customer obligations, and update the drafts before issue completion. Publish and activate them with the corresponding authorized behavior, providing required notice or consent before it takes effect.

The owner or explicitly authorized legal/business delegate under [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) approves initial policy text and material changes to data practices, rights, or obligations. Record the approved version; release approval can explicitly include that text, but a generic instruction to ship does not. Non-substantive corrections need no repeat approval.

Assess applicability before activating or materially changing children's services; health, biometric, precise-location or financial-account data; advertising data sales/sharing; messaging/marketing; payments/subscriptions; or consequential contract terms. Obtain qualified legal review for new consequential terms (such as arbitration, class-action waivers or liability caps), unusual/high-risk processing, or material unresolved obligations. Reuse valid reviewed arrangements when facts and law remain unchanged. These rules apply even without the skill; confidence is not legal approval.

Publication follows [launch.md](launch.md) and release authorization. Preparing required notices does not require a changelog or full documentation site.
